⚽ The World Cup is almost here — and I've pulled my little prediction pool out of retirement for it.

**xPool** is an app I built from the ground up — years ago as a nerdy side project so my friends and I could bet bragging rights on World Cup and Euro scorelines. It's quietly survived tournament after tournament, held together with duct tape and Python 2, and this spring I rebuilt the whole thing from scratch for 2026. 🇨🇦🇺🇸🇲🇽

This is **not** a fantasy league. You don't draft players or tinker week to week. You call the actual scorelines for whole groups *upfront* — and that part is the whole point: your predictions are locked in before a ball is kicked, before you know which favourites stroll through and which ones forgot to show up on time. No hindsight, no do-overs. And the stakes climb as you go — every prediction in the knockout rounds is worth more, so a hot streak (or a meltdown) really counts down the stretch.

It also makes you care about matches you'd never otherwise watch — once there are points on the line, even a group-stage game nobody else is following becomes appointment viewing (looking at you, Cape Verde 👀).

No money, just bragging rights and a leaderboard that gets *very* personal by the final.
